## Description — English

```
WRD (wird) — your daily companion of remembrance.

Your wird is your light: every devotion you complete kindles a lamp in your mishkāt — an illuminated record that never goes dark. No broken streaks, no guilt when you miss a day; your light remains, and tonight we continue.

Four gates for your day:
۞ Morning wird — after Fajr until sunrise
۞ After-prayer awrad — a gentle reminder after each prayer, on your city's times
۞ Night wird — evening and before sleep
۞ Situational awrad — eating, travel, home, and daily moments

Inside:
۞ A trusted adhkar library — every supplication with its source
۞ Compose your own wird: your text, your count, your dedication
۞ One-tap tasbih counter with soft haptics
۞ Prayer times calculated on your device, with adhan alerts for your city
۞ A personal reminder for any wird, at the time you choose
۞ The mishkāt: your lit days in a gilded record
۞ Khatma circles: split the Qur'an's 30 parts with family or friends and track progress with a simple share code — no internet, no accounts
۞ Two faces: parchment (light) and candlelight (dark), plus a large-text mode

Our promise: completely free. No ads, no accounts, no data collection — everything stays on your device. Your location never leaves your phone; your awrad are yours alone.

A sincere prayer from your heart is all we ask 🤍
```

## App Review notes (paste into the review notes field)

```
WRD is an Arabic-first Islamic daily-devotions app (adhkar/remembrance).

- No account or login is required; all data is stored locally on device.
- The app makes no network requests. "Khatma circles" are shared by
  copy-pasting an offline text code between users — no server involved.
- Notifications: prayer-time alerts and daily devotion reminders, all
  scheduled locally (UNUserNotificationCenter). Enable them in
  الإعدادات (Settings, slider icon on the today screen top corner).
- The interface is Arabic (RTL). "اليوم" = Today tab, "المكتبة" = Library,
  "المشكاة" = the progress/light record, "الحلقات" = khatma circles.
- Religious content: classical Islamic supplications with their canonical
  sources cited beneath each item.
```
